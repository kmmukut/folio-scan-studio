    03 / ADMINISTRATION

    The guest pass.

    Guest-only rules: PDF, PNG or JPEG; max 10 MB, 20 pages, one copy, with rate limits. Signed-in users are not subject to these guest quotas.

    Job status comes from CUPS. “Done” means CUPS finished sending the job to the printer; a paper jam or empty tray is reported by the printer itself.

    PRINT QUOTAS

    Pages per month.

    A monthly page limit for members and print-only accounts; administrators have none, and guests have their own limits. Each person can also have their own limit below. Pages are counted from the print statistics, from the first of each month.

    Print statistics are switched off, so pages aren’t counted and nobody is limited. Turn them on under Statistics.

    JOBS AT ONCE

    Fair turns.

    How many exports, prints and sends each person may have waiting or running at the same time, so no one fills the queue for everyone else. Administrators have no limit; guests may have two at a time. Each person can also have their own number above.

    Changing a user's role, password or enabled status ends their active sessions. Deleting an account preserves its scans for administrators to recover. Invitation links work once, within 48 hours; password links within one hour.
    EQUIPMENT / 06

    Tools of
    the trade.

    Add, edit or remove scanners and printers right here; Folio writes the SANE configuration and sets up CUPS queues for you. Neither requires a Docker restart.

    CUPS server

    Check a network address

    Type a printer’s or scanner’s IP address or host name. Folio asks the device what it offers (printing over IPP, JetDirect or LPD; scanning over eSCL or WSD, with the exact port and path) and fills in the forms below. It only asks: nothing is printed or scanned.

    01 / SCANNERS

    Capture devices.

    Choose the default, check capabilities, or edit network endpoints.

    Discovering scanners…

    Share as AirScan

    Let Macs (Image Capture, Preview), Windows 10 and 11, Android (the Mopria Scan app) and Linux scan from a scanner without Folio, and without a driver. Switch it on with Share as AirScan on the scanner’s card. AirScan has no sign-in: anyone on your network can then scan from it. iPhones and iPads have no built-in app for it.

    Attach a detected scanner

    For USB or other devices already visible to SANE inside this container.

    Add a network scanner

    Each brand’s WSD scanners use their own port and path: leave them empty and Folio asks the scanner, or use Check a network address above. For VLAN-separated scanners, enter their reachable IP. Folio writes the configuration into persistent storage. Nothing to mount in Docker Compose. If this is your first scanner, it becomes the default automatically.

    03 / DESTINATIONS

    Send documents onward.

    Where finished documents can be sent from the workbench: Paperless-ngx, or a folder (a NAS share, or Paperless’s consume folder) mounted into Folio under /destinations.

    Add a destination

    Mount the host folder in compose.yaml, e.g. - /srv/scans:/destinations/nas, and let the user Folio runs as (PUID) write to it.

    04 / EMAIL

    Send email.

    For invitations, “Forgot your password?” and notifications. Folio sends through your mail provider or server; with Gmail, Fastmail and the like, create an app password for it.

    Links in emails point here. Folio can’t take it from the browser’s request, which anyone can fake.

    Signing in

    Notifications

    05 / DIAGNOSTICS

    System check.

    Checks storage, memory, proxy settings, every CUPS server and every scanner. Nothing is changed.

    06 / BACKUP

    Keep a copy.

    One file with your documents, accounts, settings, print statistics, and the built-in printers with their drivers. It holds your accounts and print-server logins, so keep it somewhere safe.

    Restore from a backup

    Replaces Folio’s documents, accounts and settings with the backup’s, and sets up the built-in printers it contains again. What it replaces is kept in data/.before-restore.

    RECORDS / 07

    What went
    to paper.

    Every job Folio sends to a printer is counted here: who printed, on which printer, and how much paper it took. Jobs sent to CUPS from other computers are not included, and document names are never recorded.

    Statistics are switched off, so new print jobs are not being recorded. Turn them on under “What Folio keeps” below.

    RECORD KEEPING

    What Folio keeps.

    For each job: when, who, which printer, pages, copies, sheets, colour and paper size. Never the document's name or contents. Deleting an account removes its name from these records.

    Counts start when you switch this on. Pages are as CUPS reports them; some printers report none. Needs the print server’s administrator login (the built-in one has it).

    Only administrators can see this page. The numbers are what Folio sent to the printer: jobs cancelled in Folio are left out, but pages lost to a paper jam are not.
