03 / ADMINISTRATION

The guest pass.

Guest-only rules: PDF, PNG or JPEG; max 10 MB, 20 pages, one copy, with rate limits. Signed-in users are not subject to these guest quotas.

Job status comes from CUPS. “Done” means CUPS finished sending the job to the printer; a paper jam or empty tray is reported by the printer itself.

to paper.

Every job Folio sends to a printer is counted here: who printed, on which printer, and how much paper it took. Jobs sent to CUPS from other computers are not included, and document names are never recorded.

What Folio keeps.

For each job: when, who, which printer, pages, copies, sheets, colour and paper size. Never the document's name or contents. Deleting an account removes its name from these records.
